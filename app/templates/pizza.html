Our Story

Fuoco (Italian for "fire") was born from a backyard pizza oven and a dream. Chef Marco spent two years in Naples learning the art of Neapolitan pizza before bringing it home.

Every pie is hand-stretched, never rolled. Every ingredient is sourced with intention. Every bite should make you close your eyes.
