Our Story

Golden Crumb started in 2018 in a tiny kitchen with one oven and a dream. Founder Maria Torres learned to bake from her grandmother in Oaxaca, blending traditional Mexican baking with French pastry techniques.

Today, we're still small on purpose — because the best things are made with patience, not assembly lines.

"Every loaf tells a story. Ours starts at 4am."
