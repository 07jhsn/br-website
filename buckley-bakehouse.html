Our Story

Baked with
Purpose & Pride

The Buckley Bakehouse was born from a simple belief: that good bread should be honest, and good baking should bring people together.

Every loaf, every pastry, every cake starts with quality ingredients and proper technique — no cutting corners, no mass production. Just the craft of baking done right, right here in Swadlincote.

Whether you're picking up your weekly loaf, ordering a birthday cake, or just treating yourself to something sweet — you're always welcome here.
